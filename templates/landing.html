How It Works

From Speech to Analysis

Linguahe combines speech recognition, linguistic rules, semantic analysis, and artificial intelligence model to process spoken language.

01

Record Speech

The user records Tagalog speech through the Linguahe web interface.

02

AI Analysis

The system transcribes and analyzes the speech using Rule-Augmented Algorithm, SBERT, and artificial neural networks (ANN).

03

View Results

The processed information is presented as linguistic analysis results for the user.

About Linguahe

Exploring Regional Language Variation

Linguahe is a web-based linguistic analysis system developed to examine possible differences in spoken Tagalog Dialect across CALABARZON region.

The system uses a combination of rule-based linguistic analysis and artificial intelligence techniques to process speech and provide linguistic observations.
