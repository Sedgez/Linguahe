How to Use Linguahe

Follow these steps to analyze your speech.

Step 1: Prepare Your Speech

Linguahe analyzes spoken Filipino language through your microphone. Prepare a short and clear Filipino sentence before starting the recording.

Tips for better results:

  • Speak clearly and naturally.
  • Use a quiet environment when possible.
  • Keep your microphone close enough to capture your voice.
  • Keep your sentence within the allowed word limit.

Step 2: Enable Noise Suppression

If you are recording in an environment with background noise, enable the Noise Suppression option before recording.

When should you enable it?

  • When there is background conversation.
  • When there is fan or air-conditioning noise.
  • When there is traffic or environmental noise.

For a quiet environment, you may leave this option disabled.

Step 3: Record Your Speech

Click the 🎤 microphone button to begin recording.

⚠️ Maximum recording time: 20 seconds

Step 4: Review the Transcription

After recording, Linguahe automatically processes the audio and converts your speech into text.

Check the Speech / Text Input area.

Your transcribed sentence will appear in the text box. Make sure that the transcription accurately represents what you said.

If no audio is detected, record your sentence again and make sure you are speaking clearly into the microphone.

Step 5: View the Analysis Results

Once processing is complete, Linguahe displays the linguistic analysis in the Linguistic Analysis Results section.

The analysis may include:

  • Diction and language patterns
  • Sentence-final particle patterns
  • Speech-related characteristics
  • Intonation characteristics
  • Emotion classification
  • Possible regional linguistic indicators

Step 6: Listen, Download, or Start Again

After receiving your results, you can use the available controls to continue working with your analysis.

Listen to Text — converts the displayed text into spoken audio.

Download Report — saves your transcript and linguistic analysis as a report.

Clear — clears the current session and allows you to start again.

You now know how to use Linguahe!

About Linguahe WebApp

Linguahe is a web-based linguistic analysis system designed to detect possible cross-cultural communication differences among Tagalog dialects spoken in the CALABARZON Region.

Rule-Augmented Algorithm

  • Diction Pattern Analysis
  • Sentence-final Particle Detection
  • Speech Rate Analysis
  • Intonation Detection

Sentence-BERT (SBERT)

  • Semantic Similarity Analysis
  • Reference Sentence Comparison

Artificial Neural Network (ANN)
